Solutions

There are two main responses: reduce the causes, and prepare for the changes already happening.

Mitigation

Mitigation means cutting greenhouse gas emissions. Examples include renewable energy, better insulation, electric transport, and protecting forests.

Adaptation

Adaptation means preparing for change. Examples include flood defences, heat-resistant buildings, and drought-tolerant crops.

What you can do

Travel, food and energy choices all matter. So does learning, talking about the issue, and getting involved in your school or community.

Key terms

  • Mitigation: reducing the causes of climate change.
  • Adaptation: adjusting to the effects of climate change.
  • Renewable energy: energy from sources that are naturally replenished, such as wind and solar.
