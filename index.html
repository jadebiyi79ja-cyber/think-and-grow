Climate basics

What is the greenhouse effect?

Some gases in the atmosphere trap heat that would otherwise escape to space. This natural effect keeps Earth warm enough for life.

Weather or climate?

Weather is what happens day to day. Climate is the pattern over many years.

Read the full topic

Causes

Why is it changing now?

Burning coal, oil and gas releases extra carbon dioxide, which strengthens the greenhouse effect.

Other sources

Farming, land clearance and waste also release greenhouse gases such as methane.

Read the full topic

Impacts

Who is affected?

People, wildlife and places around the world, including the UK, through changes such as heatwaves, flooding and rising seas.

Why does it matter to me?

Choices made in the next few decades shape the world you will live and work in.

Read the full topic

Solutions

Mitigation or adaptation?

Mitigation cuts greenhouse gas emissions. Adaptation prepares communities for changes already happening.

What can one person do?

Travel, food and energy choices all count, and so does talking about it and voting when you can.
