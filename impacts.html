Impacts

Climate change affects people and nature in different ways in different places, including in the UK.

Rising temperatures

Heatwaves become more likely and more intense. This affects health, farming and how cities need to be designed.

Water and weather

A warmer atmosphere holds more moisture, which can lead to heavier rain and flooding in some places. Other regions face longer droughts.

Sea level and ecosystems

Melting ice and warming oceans raise sea levels, which threatens coastal communities. Wildlife may need to move or adapt as habitats change.

Key terms

  • Sea level rise: an increase in the average height of the ocean.
  • Ecosystem: a community of living things and their environment.
  • Extreme weather: weather far from the usual pattern for a place.
