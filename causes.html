Causes

Human activities are the main reason the climate is changing faster than it did in the past.

Fossil fuels

Burning coal, oil and natural gas for electricity, heating and transport releases carbon dioxide. Because it stays in the atmosphere for a long time, it builds up.

Land use and farming

Clearing forests removes trees that store carbon. Farming animals and growing rice release methane, and some fertilisers release nitrous oxide.

Industry and waste

Making cement and steel produces emissions, and waste in landfill releases methane as it breaks down.

Key terms

  • Emissions: greenhouse gases released into the atmosphere.
  • Fossil fuel: a fuel formed from ancient plants and animals, such as coal, oil or gas.
  • Carbon sink: something that absorbs more carbon than it releases, such as a forest.
